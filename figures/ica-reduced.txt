Absolute scores are not directly comparable with the original figure because its retired GPT-3.5/GPT-4 interpreters are replaced here. Each Fixed ICA curve applies fitting-data sign orientations to the corresponding components. Solid lines denote full-budget fits and dashed lines reduced-budget fits.
